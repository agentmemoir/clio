`clio consolidate` and the MCP `consolidate` tool refuse a bank name that no bank is registered under, reporting `not_found` and exiting 1, instead of reporting a completed wave and leaving a bank row plus `hub_distill_enqueue` and `hub_distill_complete` audit events keyed by the mistyped name. A script that consolidated a bank before that bank's first write now fails with `not_found` and exit 1, so the bank must exist first; `--bank default` and an enabled shared bank are accepted without a lookup.
