Restores now verify copy custody before placing a copy, and refuse when no custody record backs the copy, when the copy's digest does not match the recorded digest, or when the restorer is not the receiver the record names. `clio backup create` and the restore flow's pre-restore snapshot record that custody row beside the journal, and the create fails when the record cannot be written. Backups and archives created before this change carry no custody record, so restoring one refuses with "no copy-custody record backs this restore" unless a record is seeded; this change adds no backfill.
