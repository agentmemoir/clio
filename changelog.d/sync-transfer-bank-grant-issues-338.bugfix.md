The MCP sync tools now resolve the caller's credential before any bank data or custody is read: `sync_push`, `sync_pull`, `sync_status`, `sync_serve`, and `sync_ack_skip` refuse with `forbidden` when the run, report, served set, or acknowledged mutation would touch a bank the credential's list does not name or a `shared` bank the deployment has not enabled, and the transfer result names the credential that presented it; unbound local calls keep their argument contract, where an unknown `actor` is `invalid_argument`, an empty bank is `missing_bank`, and naming `shared` without the deployment gate is `forbidden`.
