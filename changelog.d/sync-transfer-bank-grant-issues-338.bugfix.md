The MCP sync transfer tools `sync_push` and `sync_pull` now resolve the caller's credential before any transfer runs: a credential bound to a bank list is refused with `forbidden` when the transfer would touch a bank the list does not name, whether the caller passed it in `banks` or the default bank resolved to it, and the transfer result names the credential that presented it.
