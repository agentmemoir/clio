`clio remember`, `clio batch`, and `clio import` leave the items they just wrote searchable by the next command, so an older index backlog larger than one drain batch can no longer hide them from `clio recall` until an operator rebuilds the index; the `store`, `batch`, and `import` results now carry a `written` list naming the bank and id of each item they stored, which is what the one-shot drain indexes.
