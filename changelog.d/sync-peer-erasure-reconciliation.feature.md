An enabled sync peer can no longer bring an erased subject back: a reconnecting peer refuses to serve or accept data for a denied subject's banks, and a duplicate or interrupted denial delivery reconciles once without a second effect. A peer's denial is durable: a required peer settles only through its own durable acknowledgement or a disposition recorded in the peer lifecycle state with evidence, and an unresolved required peer refuses technical completion while an offline peer no longer blocks the local erasure; a deployment with peers disabled records peer enablement as not applicable rather than unknown; and a retention exception or an unknown disposition is reported as a partial or refused erasure rather than a clean result. The operator commands that would open the peer snapshot, deliver a denial, acknowledge it, or record a disposition are not part of this change.
