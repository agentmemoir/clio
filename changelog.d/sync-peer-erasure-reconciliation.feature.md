An enabled sync peer can no longer bring an erased subject back. An erasure freezes the explicitly enabled peers it was opened with, keeps each peer's denial pending until that peer reports a durable denial of its own, and refuses technical completion while a required peer is unresolved; an offline peer no longer blocks the local erasure. A reconnecting peer refuses to serve or accept data for a denied subject's banks, a duplicate or interrupted denial delivery reconciles once, and an operator's explicit peer disposition is recorded with its evidence, so a retention exception or an unknown disposition is reported as a partial or refused erasure rather than a clean result.
