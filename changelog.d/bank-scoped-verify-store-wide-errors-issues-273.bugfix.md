`clio ops verify --bank <bank>` now reports the error codes other banks carry in its `notes`, and exits non-zero while the store holds an error finding outside the named bank, instead of printing `ok:true` over a store that is not clean. A bank-scoped pass run through a bank-bound credential reads no other bank and carries no such note, as `clio ops doctor --bank` already did.
