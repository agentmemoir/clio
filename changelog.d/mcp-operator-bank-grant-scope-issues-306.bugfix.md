An authenticated MCP caller granted one bank could omit `bank` and make `repair`, `reindex`, `verify`, `doctor`, or `diagnose` act on another bank or on the whole store. These operator tools now forward only a scope bounded by the caller's grant and refuse a bank it does not cover, so for a bound caller an omitted `bank` reaches the granted bank instead of the whole store, while local calls without a credential keep their whole-store behavior.
