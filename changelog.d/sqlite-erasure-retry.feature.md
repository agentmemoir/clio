A confirmed local erasure now runs as one durable action: the all-bank confirmation is rechecked before any journal denial or key loss, a failed intent or a hard effect error leaves the action pending rather than claiming an outcome, an unresolved peer keeps completion pending, and a later pass retries only the steps still outstanding — a destroyed key whose purge never committed stays pending until the purge commits — without ever recreating a lost key. Erasing one subject in a shared bank leaves the other subject's items and key untouched, and a shared derived summary stops exposing the erased content.
